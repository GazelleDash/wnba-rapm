RAPM = points per 100 possessions vs. league average, ridge-regularized over play-by-play stints. ORAPM/DRAPM split that into offense and defense; the factor columns decompose each side into shooting, turnover and rebounding value.
Career-Decay weights every possession by an exponential half-life, so recent play counts most.
Percentile subscripts rank within the currently qualified pool — change the season, window, team or minimum-possession filters and the percentiles are recomputed against whoever still qualifies.
